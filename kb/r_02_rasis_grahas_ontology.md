# Rasis & Grahas Ontology, Dignities & Friendships

## 1. Core Concepts & Definitions

* **Rasis vs. Grahas:** **Rasis (Signs)** represent *situations, environments, and forces* entering a native's life (limbs of Lord Vishnu). **Grahas (Planets)** represent *individual beings, actors, and active agencies* playing roles in a native's life (incarnations / Avataras of Lord Vishnu).
* **Dignity Mood Analogy (PVR p. 47–48):**
  * **Own Rasi (Swakshetra):** Like one's *home*—natural, comfortable, authentic.
  * **Moolatrikona:** Like one's *office*—formal, duty-driven, authoritative.
  * **Exaltation (Uchcha):** Like a *favorite picnic/party*—excited, exuberant, elevated.
  * **Debilitation (Neecha):** Like one's *worst party*—unhappy, uncomfortable, constrained.
* **Trigunas:** **Sattwa** = purity and truthfulness (state of being true, *not* passive weakness); **Rajas** = passion, energy, and ego-driven action; **Tamas** = darkness, inertia, depravity.

## 2. Deterministic Rules & Formulas

### 2.1 Natural Benefics vs. Natural Malefics
* **Always Natural Benefics (Saumya / Subha):** `Jupiter`, `Venus`.
* **Always Natural Malefics (Kroora / Paapa):** `Sun`, `Mars`, `Saturn`, `Rahu`, `Ketu`.
* **Conditional:**
  * `Moon`: Natural Benefic during **Sukla Paksha** (waxing, $0^\circ$–$180^\circ$ ahead of Sun); Natural Malefic during **Krishna Paksha** (waning, $180^\circ$–$360^\circ$).
  * `Mercury`: Natural Benefic when alone or conjoined with more natural benefics; Natural Malefic when conjoined with more natural malefics.

### 2.2 Planetary Relationships (Panchadha Maitri / 5-Fold Friendship)
1. **Natural Relationship (Naisargika Maitri):** From a planet's Moolatrikona sign, the lords of the `2nd, 4th, 5th, 8th, 9th, 12th` signs AND the lord of its exaltation sign are **Natural Friends**. Lords of other signs are **Natural Enemies**. A planet qualifying as both (via owning two signs) is **Neutral**. (See Table 7.)
2. **Temporary Relationship (Tatkaala Maitri — chart-specific):**
   * Planets in the `2nd, 3rd, 4th, 10th, 11th, 12th` Rasis from a planet's Rasi = **Temporary Friends**.
   * Planets in the `1st, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th` Rasis from a planet's Rasi = **Temporary Enemies**.
3. **Compound Relationship (Table 8):** Combine Natural + Temporary:
   * `Natural Friend + Temp Friend` $\rightarrow$ **Adhimitra** (Great Friend)
   * `Natural Friend + Temp Enemy` $\rightarrow$ **Sama** (Neutral)
   * `Natural Neutral + Temp Friend` $\rightarrow$ **Mitra** (Friend)
   * `Natural Neutral + Temp Enemy` $\rightarrow$ **Satru** (Enemy)
   * `Natural Enemy + Temp Friend` $\rightarrow$ **Sama** (Neutral)
   * `Natural Enemy + Temp Enemy` $\rightarrow$ **Adhisatru** (Bitter Enemy)
   * *Global Rule:* Whenever any later rule says "friendly house" or "inimical house", it strictly means **Compound Relationship** (`Mitra/Adhimitra` vs. `Satru/Adhisatru`).

### 2.3 Exceptions & Edge Cases
* **Moolatrikona Degree Splits (PVR p. 46–47):**
  * **Sun in Le:** $0^\circ$–$20^\circ$ Moolatrikona; $20^\circ$–$30^\circ$ Own sign.
  * **Moon in Ta:** $0^\circ$–$3^\circ$ Exaltation; $3^\circ$–$30^\circ$ Moolatrikona.
  * **Mars in Ar:** $0^\circ$–$12^\circ$ Moolatrikona; $12^\circ$–$30^\circ$ Own sign *(Note: PVR p. 46 prints "Le" by typo; Mars's MT is `Ar` per Table 6).*
  * **Mercury in Vi:** $0^\circ$–$15^\circ$ Exaltation; $15^\circ$–$20^\circ$ Moolatrikona; $20^\circ$–$30^\circ$ Own sign.
  * **Jupiter in Sg:** $0^\circ$–$10^\circ$ Moolatrikona; $10^\circ$–$30^\circ$ Own sign.
  * **Venus in Li:** $0^\circ$–$15^\circ$ Moolatrikona; $15^\circ$–$30^\circ$ Own sign.
  * **Saturn in Aq:** $0^\circ$–$20^\circ$ Moolatrikona; $20^\circ$–$30^\circ$ Own sign.
* **Co-Lordship:** Scorpio is co-owned by `Mars` & `Ketu`; Aquarius is co-owned by `Saturn` & `Rahu`.
* **Seershodaya vs. Prishthodaya Dasa Timing:** Planets in Seershodaya signs tend to give results in the **first half** of their Dasas; planets in Prishthodaya signs in the **second half**.

## 5. Interpretive Guidelines & Modifiers

* **Elemental Psychology:** Apply Rasi elements to houses—e.g., 5th house in Fire = aggressive/determined emotions; Earth = balanced/logical; Air = wandering/unstable; Water = imaginative/creative.
* **Dietary & Health Prevention (PVR 3.2.14):** The 2nd house shows preferred tastes. During an adverse Shoola Dasa of a planet, reduce the taste ruled by that planet (e.g., cut salt during Moon suffering to guard blood pressure; cut sweets during Jupiter suffering to guard fat/metabolic issues).
