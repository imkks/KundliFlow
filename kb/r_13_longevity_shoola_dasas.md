# Longevity Estimation, Marakas, Rudra/Maheswara & Shoola Dasas

## 1. Core Concepts & Definitions

* **Houses of Life vs. Death:** `3rd` (vitality) and `8th` (longevity) are houses of life. Their `12th` houses (`2nd` and `7th`) are **Maraka Sthaanas** (killer houses); their lords and closely afflicting malefics are **Maraka Grahas**.
* **Charts for Death/Crisis:** Primary = `D1` (physical existence); Secondary = `D11` (Rudramsa — destruction/violence) and `D30` (Trimsamsa — punishment for evils).
* **Three Sacred Entities of Longevity:**
  * **Rudra:** The stronger 8th lord (via Table `32`) representing suffering/destruction of the physical body.
  * **Trishoola Rasis:** The 3 trines (`1st, 5th, 9th`) from **Rudra's sign** in `D1` (three spikes of Shiva's trident).
  * **Maheswara:** The `8th` lord from **`AK`** (Chara Atma Karaka), representing the soul's channel of liberation.
* **Niryaana Shoola Dasa vs. Shoola Dasa (PVR p. 294):**
  * **Niryaana Shoola Dasa** = uneven motion of **Prana** (life force); death occurs when Prana hits a **Trishoola Rasi** or strong **Maraka Rasi**.
  * **Shoola Dasa** = constant motion (`9 yrs/sign`) of **Lord Shiva**; death occurs when Shiva's force hits the **trines, `3rd`, or `8th` from `AL`** (destroying the *maya* of existence).

## 2. Deterministic Rules & Formulas

### 2.1 Rudra, Trishoola & Maheswara
1. **Rudra:** Using **Table 32** (NOT standard counting), find the `8th` lord from `Lagna` and the `8th` lord from the `7th` house. Pick the **stronger** planet (more conjoined planets $\rightarrow$ exalt/own $\rightarrow$ with exalted $\rightarrow$ more rasi aspects $\rightarrow$ higher degree).
   * *Afflicted Exception:* If the *weaker* of the two is debilitated or in an inimical sign AND conjoined/aspected by malefics (`Mars, Sat, Rahu, Ketu`), **it** becomes Rudra.
   * *Saturn-in-Lagna Note (PVR Ex. 86, p. 288):* When Saturn occupies Lagna, take the 8th house in the normal zodiacal way rather than Table `32`.
2. **Trishoola Rasis:** The sign occupied by Rudra in `D1`, plus the `5th` and `9th` from it.
3. **Maheswara:** Lord of the `8th` house from `AK` in `D1`.
   * *Exception 1 (Own/Exalt):* If that `8th` lord is in own or exaltation sign, take the **stronger of the `8th` and `12th` lords from that planet**.
   * *Exception 2 (Nodes):* If `Rahu` or `Ketu` joins `AK` OR the `8th` from `AK`, take the **`6th` lord from `AK`** (8th anti-zodiacally) instead.
   * *Exception 3 (Node Result):* If `Rahu` becomes Maheswara $\rightarrow$ substitute **Mercury**; if `Ketu` $\rightarrow$ substitute **Jupiter**.

### 2.2 Method of Three Pairs (Longevity Category & Paramayush)
Evaluate the sign modalities (`Movable, Fixed, Dual`) of 3 pairs:
1. `Lagna Lord` & `8th Lord` *(8th house via Table 32)*.
2. `Moon` & `Saturn`.
3. `Lagna` & `Hora Lagna (HL)`.
* Map each pair via **Table 33** to `{Short: 0–36y, Middle: 36–72y, Long: 72–108y}`.
* **Combine:** If 2 or 3 pairs agree, that category wins (look up **Paramayush** in Table `34`). If all 3 disagree (1 Short, 1 Middle, 1 Long), use **Pair 3 (`Lagna + HL`)**—*unless* Moon is in `1st` or `7th`, in which case use **Pair 2 (`Moon + Saturn`)**.

### 2.3 Eighth Lord Method (Confirmatory)
Take the stronger of `1st` and `7th` as reference. Check placement of its `8th` lord from it:
* `Kendra` $\rightarrow$ Long life | `Panaphara` $\rightarrow$ Middle life | `Apoklima` $\rightarrow$ Short life.

### 2.4 Niryaana Shoola Dasa (Prana Motion)
1. **Start:** Stronger of **`2nd` and `8th`** houses.
2. **Direction:** **Forward** if starting sign is **Odd**; **Backward** if **Even** *(Saturn in starting sign overrides to Forward)*.
3. **Fixed Dasa Lengths:** `Movable = 7 yrs`, `Fixed = 8 yrs`, `Dual = 9 yrs`. *(Thumbrule: every 3 consecutive Dasas sum to `24` years.)*
4. **Antardasas:** Use Narayana Dasa `AD` rules (`R-12`).
5. **Trigger Rules:** Primary `MD` = Trishoola Rasi falling in the native's longevity category (else Rasi with Rudra in its `12th`). Primary `AD` = `6th, 7th, 8th, or 12th` from `MD` Rasi, OR a Rasi casting Rasi Drishti on the **`D9` sign** of the `8th` lord from the `MD` Rasi.

### 2.5 Shoola Dasa (Shiva Motion — Self & Near Relatives)
1. **Start (Self):** Stronger of `1st` and `7th` house. Always progresses **Forward (zodiacally)** at exactly **`9` years per sign** (`9` months per `AD`, starting from stronger of `MD` Rasi & `7th`, always forward).
   * *Compressed Mundane Shoola Dasa:* Compress `108` years to term length (e.g., 5-year PM term $\rightarrow$ `5` months per Dasa).
2. **Longevity Buckets:** Dasas `1–4` (`0–36y` Short); Dasas `5–8` (`36–72y` Middle); Dasas `9–12` (`72–108y` Long).
3. **Trigger Rules (Self):** Trines from `AL`, OR afflicted `3rd` / `8th` (via Table `32`) from `AL`. **Protection Rule:** A Rasi occupied or aspected by **`AK` or `Jupiter`** generally does NOT kill (unless that planet is Rudra).
4. **Death of Near Relatives (PVR Ch. 23.5):**
   * Start Shoola Dasa from the stronger of the relative's house and its `7th` (`Pitri = 9th/3rd`, `Bhratri = 3rd/9th`, `Matri = 4th/10th`, `Dara = 7th/1st`, `Putra = 5th/11th`).
   * Trigger = **Trines from the relative's Sthira Karaka (`R-05`)** or trines from the corresponding Bhava Arudha (`A9`, `A4`, etc.).
   * *Nature of Death:* Check `3rd` from the Sthira Karaka (malefics/Rahu/Mars = violent/accidental; benefics = peaceful).

### 2.6 Mathematical Ayurdaya Summary (Charak Ch. XXIII — Research Only)
* **Pindayu** (use if Sun strongest): Exaltation base years `Sun 19, Moon 25, Mars 15, Merc 12, Jup 15, Ven 21, Sat 20` (half at debilitation), reduced by Astangata (combust $1/2$), Shatru-Kshetra ($1/3$), Chakrapata (visible half), and Krurodaya (malefic in Lagna).
* **Amshayu** (use if Lagna strongest): Total minutes $\div 200 \bmod 12$ + Bharanas (`×3` exalt/retro; `×2` own/Vargottama) - single largest Harana.
* **Nisargayu** (use if Moon strongest): Fixed `Moon 1, Mars 2, Merc 9, Ven 20, Jup 18, Sun 20, Sat 50`.
* *(Both Charak and PVR warn mathematical Ayurdaya is unreliable in practice.)*

   * **Long Life** (`72–108y`). Shoola starts `Ar`; `AL=Aq` (protected by `AK Jupiter` in `Aq`) $\rightarrow$ `8th` from `AL` via Table `32` = `Cp` (afflicted by Rahu, age `81–90`) $\rightarrow$ Died age 83 in `Cp` Dasa.
