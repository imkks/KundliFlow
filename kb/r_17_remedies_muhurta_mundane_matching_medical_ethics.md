# Remedies, Muhurta, Mundane, Matching, Medical & Ethics

## 1. Core Concepts & Definitions

* **Karma vs. Free Will (PVR Ch. 34.1):** Birth karma fixes the *broad pattern* of life, while free will and remedial measures modulate the *exact intensity and details* (e.g., turning a major accident into a minor scrape).
* **Muhurta (Electional Astrology):** Choosing an auspicious start time for a venture. Crucially, define the **true start moment** (e.g., tying the *mangal sutra* / pronouncing vows for marriage; boiling milk on the stove for housewarming; beginning actual work for a new job).
* **Mundane Astrology:** Forecasting collective fortunes of nations/groups using Independence charts (start of a new order), Lunar New Year/Month charts, Solar Ingress charts, and Swearing-in charts.
* **Ashtakoota Matching (Charak Ch. XXVII):** Traditional 36-point Moon-Nakshatra compatibility system; must be supplemented with full natal chart & longevity parity (`R-13`) in modern contexts.
* **Prashna (Horary — Charak Ch. XXVIII):** Answering a query from the exact moment the question is asked without needing a birth chart.
* **Jyotishi Ethics (PVR Ch. 37):** Astrology is a spiritual service, not fear-based commerce. Never scare clients with fatalistic predictions; deliver cautions gently and always pair them with constructive action or remedies.

## 2. Deterministic Rules & Formulas

### 2.1 Remedial Measures Framework (PVR Ch. 34)
Four progressive tiers:
1. **Common-Sense Precautions:** Drive cautiously during accident periods; exercise restraint with superiors during authority conflicts.
2. **Gemstones & Metals (Table `77`):**
   * **Selection Rule:** Prescribe **ONLY for Functional Benefics** (`R-07`) or Yogakarakas (in `D1` or the target Varga, e.g., `D10` Yogakaraka for career), especially if weak/ill-placed (e.g., in `8th`) or running a favorable Dasa/Yoga.
   * **Contraindications:** **NEVER** prescribe gemstones for functional malefics or for active Maraka planets during Maraka Dasas.
   * **Combining Gems:** Allowed if both planets are friends, functional benefics, and form a Yoga (e.g., Virgo Lagna `Merc + Ven` $\rightarrow$ Emerald + Diamond).
   * **Finger Assignment:** Traditional (`Jup=Index, Sat=Middle, Sun=Ring, Merc/Ven=Little`) OR Purushartha-based (`Ring=Dharma, Middle=Artha/career, Little=Kama/marriage, Index=Moksha [left ringless]`). Wear first on the planet's weekday when it is transit-strong.
3. **Good Deeds, Charity Grains & Fasting:**
   * **Planetary Charity/Actions:** `Sun`: temple donation; `Moon`: music institute / helping a lady; `Mars`: exercise / school gym; `Mercury`: scholars' org; `Jupiter`: learned priest / cows; `Venus`: poetry / artists; `Saturn`: manual labor / helping laborers; `Rahu`: research org / pilgrimage; `Ketu`: meditation.
   * **Charity Grains:** `Sun: wheat`, `Moon: rice`, `Mars: toor daal`, `Mercury: moong daal`, `Jupiter: chickpeas`, `Venus: whitish grain`, `Saturn: sesame seeds`, `Rahu: black gram (urad) daal`.
   * **Arudha Fasting Rule:** Fast on the weekday ruled by **`UL` lord** (`D1`) to mitigate marital troubles; fast on **`A10` lord's** weekday to mitigate career troubles.
4. **Mantras & Deity Propitiation (Table `78`):**
   * *Mantra vs. Stotra:* Mantras require exact pronunciation/intonation; Stotras (e.g., 108 names) require devotion and are safe for all.
   * *Standard Repetition Counts:* `Sun: 6,000`, `Moon: 10,000`, `Mars: 7,000`, `Mercury: 17,000`, `Jupiter: 16,000`, `Venus: 20,000`, `Saturn: 19,000`, `Rahu: 18,000`, `Ketu: 7,000`.
   * *Target-Varga Lagna Lord:* Worship the deity of the Lagna lord of the Varga you wish to uplift (e.g., `D20` Lagna lord for spirituality, `D16` for comforts).
   * *Moksha / Ishta Devata:* Worship the deity of the **strongest planet occupying, owning, or aspecting the `12th` from `AK` in `D9`**.
   * *Composite Deity Synthesis:* Blend planetary influences (`Merc/Sat` = Vaishnava; `Sun` = Shaiva; `Moon/Ven` = Devi/Goddess; `Mars` = fiery; `Rahu` = fierce/aggressive $\rightarrow$ e.g., `Rahu + Mars` aspected by `Sat/Merc` = **Ugra Narasimha**; `Mars + Sat` = **Hanuman**).

### 2.2 Muhurta Selection Rules (PVR Ch. 36 & Charak Ch. XXVI)
1. Check 5 Panchanga limbs (`Tithi, Vaara, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana`) + auspicious **Tara** (`R-14`) from native's Janma Nakshatra + favorable **Hora** (lord favorable to the target house & Arudha in natal chart) + natal `SAV >= 30` for Muhurta Lagna/Moon/Sun.
2. **Primary Override Rule:** Strong **Rasi + Target Varga** planetary placements (`D10` for job, `D24` for education, `D16` for vehicle, `D4` for house/real estate) AND a **favorable 1st Vimsottari Dasa** (compressed to task duration) are MORE important than rigid thumb-rules (e.g., empty 7th house).

### 2.3 Mundane Astrology Rules (PVR Ch. 35)
* **Independence Chart (New Order):** Primary chart for a nation; use Vimsottari, Narayana, Tajaka Annual, and Sudarsana Chakra Dasa.
* **Lunar New Year / Month Charts:** Cast at exact Sun-Moon conjunction in Pisces (year) or each sign (month) at the nation's capital. Free from Ayanamsha error. Shows public mood, unrest, weather, trade, calamities. Compress Vimsottari to 360d (`×3` days/yr) or 30d (`×0.25` days/yr) with **NO birth balance reduction** (full 1st Dasa runs first).
* **Solar Ingress Charts:** Sun entering Aries or Capricorn (Makara Sankranti — best in Kali Yuga) at capital. Shows politics, wars, vitality, foreign affairs. Use **compressed Narayana Dasa** (`3 × n` days). *(Warning: $1'$ Ayanamsha error = $6^\circ$ Lagna error.)*
* **Swearing-In Charts:** Compress Narayana Dasa (and Shoola Dasa) from 120 yrs (or 108 yrs) to the leader's maximum term (e.g., 5 yrs $\rightarrow$ `0.5` month per Narayana year).

### 2.4 Ashtakoota Marriage Matching & Naadi Exceptions (Charak Ch. XXVII)
8 Kootas totaling **36 points** (minimum threshold typically $\ge 18$):
`1. Varna (1)` + `2. Vashya (2)` + `3. Taara (3)` + `4. Yoni (4 — uses 28 Nakshatras incl. Abhijit)` + `5. Graha-Maitri (5)` + `6. Gana (6)` + `7. Bhakoota (7)` + `8. Naadi (8)`.
* **Standard Tri-Naadi (3 Naadis):** Aadi, Madhya, Antya (same Naadi = `0` pts, different = `8` pts).
* **Chatur-Naadi Exception (4 Naadis):** Apply when the **bride's** Nakshatra spans `3` padas in one sign and `1` in the next (`Krittika, Punarvasu, U.Phalguni, Vishakha, U.Ashadha, P.Bhadrapada` — Sun & Jupiter stars):
  * `N1`: Krittika, Magha, P.Phalguni, Jyeshtha, Moola, U.Bhadra, Revati
  * `N2`: Rohini, Ashlesha, U.Phalguni, Anuradha, P.Ashadha, P.Bhadra, Ashwini
  * `N3`: Mrigashira, Pushya, Hasta, Vishakha, U.Ashadha, Shatabhisha, Bharani
  * `N4`: Ardra, Punarvasu, Chitra, Swati, Shravana, Dhanishtha
* **Pancha-Naadi Exception (5 Naadis):** Apply when the **bride's** Nakshatra splits `2+2` padas across signs (`Mrigashira, Chitra, Dhanishtha` — Mars stars):
  * `N1`: Mrigashira, Chitra, Swati, Shatabhisha, P.Bhadra | `N2`: Ardra, Hasta, Vishakha, Dhanishtha, U.Bhadra | `N3`: Punarvasu, U.Phalguni, Anuradha, Shravana, Revati | `N4`: Pushya, P.Phalguni, Jyeshtha, U.Ashadha, Ashwini, Rohini | `N5`: Ashlesha, Magha, Moola, P.Ashadha, Bharani, Krittika.

### 2.5 Medical Astrology & Prashna Rules (Charak Ch. XXIV & XXVIII)
* **Medical Diagnosis Checklist:** (1) `D1` + exact cusps, (2) `D3`, `D9`, `D12`, `D30`, (3) Vimshottari `MD/AD/PD` (esp. birth Dasa), (4) Confirm via Yogini/Chara Dasa, Ashtakavarga, Varshaphala, Transits, Prashna.
* **Key Pathogenic Factors:** Afflicted `6th/8th/12th`, `22nd` Drekkana lord, `64th` Navamsa lord, Gandanta Moon, and **Retrograde Rule** (retrograde benefics fail to protect against disease; retrograde malefics worsen illness).
* **Prashna Kendras (Charak Ch. XXVIII):** General (`1st`=change/disturbance, `4th`=success, `7th`=return, `10th`=absence); Medical (`1st`=physician, `4th`=medicine, `7th`=disease, `10th`=patient).
