# Master Synthesis Workflows & Real-Life Few-Shot Reasoning Traces

## 1. Core Concepts & Definitions

* **The Composite Prediction Mandate (Charak Ch. I + PVR Ch. 13 & Part 6 + K.N. Rao & M.S. Mehta):** A reliable astrological prediction requires **convergence across independent layers**. Static charts define *what is possible* (innate promise); Dasas define *when the window opens*; Tajaka/Sudarsana Chakra, **Tri-Pataki Chakra & Dwi-Janma (`R-20`)**, and **K.N. Rao's Double Transit of Saturn & Jupiter (`R-19`)** confirm *the specific year/month*; Gochara/Kakshya/60-Hour charts pinpoint *the exact week or day*.
* **3-Layer Perspective Synthesis:** For every counseling query, simultaneously report:
  1. **Objective Reality** (from **Lagna** in target Varga).
  2. **Material Status / External Perception** (from **`AL`** & **Bhava Arudhas**).
  3. **Native's Subjective Feelings / Mental State** (from **Moon / Vimsottari** & **Graha Arudhas** + **K.N. Rao's Astro-Psychology diagnosis `R-19`**).

## 2. Deterministic Rules & Formulas (Master Orchestration Pipelines)

### 2.1 The 6-Step Static Analysis Pipeline (PVR Ch. 13.4.1 + K.N. Rao Checklists I–III)
Before running a domain prediction, execute **K.N. Rao's Checklists I–III (`R-19` Sec. 3.1)** to catch red flags (e.g., exalted in `D1` / debilitated in `D9`, `PAC` links, Leo/Sagittarius placements), then execute:
1. **Select Varga (`R-04`):** Map query to `D1` + domain Varga (`D10` career, `D9` marriage, `D7` children, `D24` education, `D4` home/abroad, `D16` vehicles, `D20` spirituality, `D27` temperament, `D30` crisis).
2. **Select House (`R-05`):** Identify primary house (and Bhavat Bhavam derived house) inside that Varga.
3. **Select Reference Frame (`R-05`):** Choose `Lagna` (true self/skills), `AL` (status/competition), `Paaka Lagna` (physical body/memory), `Moon` (mind), `GL/HL` (power/wealth), or **Naisargika/Chara Karaka** (when stronger than Lagna).
4. **House vs. Arudha Check (`R-06`):** Check whether the query asks about internal reality (`Bhava`) or tangible external markers (`An` — e.g., `A10` workplace, `A4` car/school, `A5` grades/awards, `A3` books, `UL` marriage).
5. **Evaluate Influences (`R-07`, `R-08`, `R-10`, `R-19`):** Inspect occupants, lord dignity/Avastha, `PAC`, Graha/Rasi Drishti, unobstructed **Argala**, **Baadhaka**, Functional Benefic/Malefic role, and **Varga `SAV`/`BAV`**.
6. **Check Yogas & Classical Rules (`R-09`, `R-19` + Charak Ch. XVII–XIX):** Detect active Raja/Dhana/Mahapurusha/Naabhasa/Tapaswi/Debilitated-`3/6/8` Raja Yogas, grade by orb & Dasavarga Amsabala, and adapt classical texts to modern contexts.

### 2.2 The 4-Stage Dynamic Timing Funnel
1. **Stage 1 — Static Promise Gate:** Confirm the event is promised in **`D1` AND Target Varga** (e.g., `D9` for marriage, `D10` for career). *If unpromised, do not force a prediction from transits or Annual Charts.*
2. **Stage 2 — Macro Window (Multi-Year to Month):** Cross-check at least **2 independent Natal Dasas**:
   * *Mind / General:* **Vimsottari Dasa** (`MD/AD/PD` + Tripod of Life + **K.N. Rao's 6-Way Dasha Evaluation & Laghu/Madhya Parashari exceptions `R-19`** + Utpanna/Kshema/Adhana check).
   * *External Event:* **Varga Narayana Dasa** (`MD/AD/PD` in the domain Varga).
   * *Domain Specialist:* **Kalachakra** (connectedness + `SAV`), **Sudasa** (wealth/status), **Drigdasa** (spirituality), or **Shoola/Niryaana Shoola** (health/risk).
3. **Stage 3 — Annual, Monthly & Double Transit Confirmation:**
   * Verify **K.N. Rao's Double Transit of Saturn & Jupiter (`R-19` Sec. 2.1)** on the target house/lord (including retrograde acting from the previous sign), gated by **Ashtakavarga (`SAV >= 25–30`)**.
   * Check **Sudarsana Chakra Dasa** (`MD` year sign evaluated in Tajaka Annual chart).
   * Cast **Tajaka Annual Chart (`D1` + Target Varga)** and apply **`R-15` + `R-20`**:
     * Run **K.N. Rao's 4-Point Varshaphala Check** (Lagna, Luminaries, `PVB` of non-Sun planets, Natal alignment).
     * Flag **Dwi-Janma Year** (`Annual_Lagna == Birth_Lagna`) and compute **Tri-Pataki Chakra** Vedha on Lagna & Moon.
     * Check **Punya Saham** first + domain **Saham** (`Vivaha, Putra, Rajya, Karma, Paradesa, Vidya`), **Ithasala/Eesarpha** with Year Lord/Lagna Lord, **Muntha** (+ half-year cross-check), and **Varsheswara**.
     * Narrow to month/week via **Varsha Narayana Dasa**, **Patyayini Dasa**, **Mudda Dasa**, and **Annual Yogini Dasa** (+ **Tajaka Monthly Chart**).
4. **Stage 4 — Exact Day/Hour Trigger:**
   * Verify **Gochara** (Jupiter/Saturn/Dasa-lord/Mars transits over natal houses/lords/Sahams) + **Divisional Transits** (`Natal Varga × Transit D1` & `Natal D1 × Transit Varga`).
   * Check **Kakshya/PAV**, **Vedha**, **Murthi**, **Latta**, **SBC**, and (for 1–2 day outcomes) **Tajaka Sixty-Hour Chart**.
