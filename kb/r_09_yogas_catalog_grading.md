# Planetary Yogas Catalog, Raja Yoga Grading & Balarishta

## 1. Core Concepts & Definitions

* **Yoga:** A specific planetary configuration producing targeted life results. Most Yogas manifest primarily during the **Dasas** of participating planets/signs, **except Naabhasa Yogas**, which operate continuously throughout life.
* **Varga Application:** Many Yogas (especially Ravi, Chandra, Raja, Dhana, Budha-Aditya) apply inside Divisional Charts (`D9`, `D10`, `D20`, `D24`), adapting their results to the Varga's domain.
* **Modern Adaptation (Desa–Kaala–Paatra):** Classical "king" results map to modern equivalents (Prime Minister, CEO, Governor, top leader in one's field).

## 2. Deterministic Rules & Formulas

### 2.1 Raja Yoga Core Association & Magnitude Grading (PVR Ch. 11.7.1–11.7.2)
* **Basic Raja Yoga:** Association between any **Kendra lord (`1, 4, 7, 10`)** and any **Trikona lord (`1, 5, 9`)** via: (1) Conjunction, (2) Mutual Graha Drishti, or (3) Parivartana (sign exchange).
  * *Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga:* Association of **`9th` lord** and **`10th` lord** (highest basic Raja Yoga).
  * *Vipareeta Raja Yoga (VRY):* Lords of Dusthanas (`6, 8, 12`) placed in Dusthanas (ideally all 3 together without other planets, or in `3rd`/`11th`). Gives tremendous rise after initial struggle. If VRY planets conjoin/aspect Lagna lord, power becomes permanent.
* **4-Factor Magnitude Grading Formula:**
  1. **Affliction Check:** Free from close conjunction/aspect of functional malefics.
  2. **Orb Check:** Conjunction/aspect orb $\le 5^\circ$–$6^\circ$ for peak fructification.
  3. **Dignity/State Check:** Not combust, debilitated, in inimical sign, or bad Avastha.
  4. **Dasavarga Amsabala Tier (`R-04`):** Count of own/exalt/MT placements in the 10 Dasavarga charts for both planets:
     * `0–1`: Ordinary | `2 (Paarijaata)`: Good ruler | `3 (Uttama)`: Great assets | `4 (Gopura)`: Respected by kings | `5 (Simhaasana)`: Emperor/world-level | `6–9 (Paaraavata–Airaavata)`: Divine/Avataric.
  5. **Amplifier:** `AL` and `A7` (Darapada) NOT in mutual `2/12` or `6/8` positions makes Raja Yogas far more effective.

### 2.2 Naabhasa Yoga Precedence Rules (Charak Ch. XX)
* Exclude **Rahu and Ketu** (7 planets only).
* **Precedence when overlapping:** `Aakriti` overrides `Aashraya` and `Sankhya`. `Sankhya` applies *only* if no other Naabhasa Yoga forms—**except `Gola` Yoga**, which overrides `Aashraya`.

## 5. Interpretive Guidelines & Modifiers

* **Partial Conditions:** In multi-condition Yogas, partial results can manifest if core pillars are strong, but never promise full classical results unless the participating planets have strong Shadbala, clean orb, and good Dasavarga Amsabala.
